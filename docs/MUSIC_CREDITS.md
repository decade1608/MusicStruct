# Included music and attribution

Original recordings by Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com), licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

- Carefree — https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/index.html?isrc=USUAN1400037
- Cipher — https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/index.html?isrc=USUAN1100844
- The Builder — https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/index.html?isrc=USUAN1400016

Recordings are unmodified. Credits and links appear in the player. Publisher tempo/duration and upload year are recorded in backend/data/licensed.json. The year is the publication/upload year, not a claim about album release. Instrumental is a local broad category. Energy, danceability, valence and optional musical features are unmeasured defaults required by the legacy schema, not extracted or publisher-verified values. The interface flags this; feature-based comparisons are illustrative. Sections and instrument clips are empty rather than fabricated.

Downloads are under .local/licensed-downloads; playback copies are managed by the audio API under .local/media. Back up media alongside PostgreSQL. The install script exports the collection first and removes only unchanged synthetic fixtures without attached audio. It preserves edited fixtures and user recordings. Deletions can also be undone through History. Automated tests continue to use synthetic fixtures in disposable databases.
